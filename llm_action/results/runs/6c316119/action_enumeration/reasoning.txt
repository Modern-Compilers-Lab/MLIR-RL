- **Cache hierarchy (L1/L2/L3)**: multi-level tiling essential for reuse and bandwidth.
- **No SMT**: each thread is a full core; synchronization costs are non-trivial.

## Primary Optimization Intents

For loop-nest dominated kernels on this CPU, three high-impact intents emerge:

### Intent 1: **Cache-Aware Data Reuse & Locality**
Loop nests have nested memory access patterns with varying stride/reuse. Multi-level tiling (L1, L2, L3-aware) is a canonical, high-impact transformation. This intent captures **blocking and permutation** to reshape iteration space for spatial/temporal locality.

### Intent 2: **Vectorization & SIMD Exposure**
Modern CPUs require explicit vectorization to achieve peak arithmetic throughput. The iteration space and memory strides must be restructured to align with vector width (4–8 lanes for FP64/FP32 on AVX2). Includes both **innermost-loop vectorization** and **data layout / packing** to enable contiguous SIMD loads.

### Intent 3: **Parallelization & Work Distribution**
Multi-core CPUs benefit from explicit loop-level parallelism. Outer loops (especially parallel iterations) must be distributed across cores while respecting NUMA and avoiding contention. This includes **coarse-grain outer-loop parallelization**, **work distribution strategies**, and **synchronization** minimization.
