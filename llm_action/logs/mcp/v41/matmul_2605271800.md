- VectorizationPar is the strongest single action (2.23ms, 146.8x speedup) — combines parallelism + vectorization

### Phase 3 Key Observations
- **Promotion + Vectorization = LLVM failure.** Any schedule combining Promotion (memref) with VecSeq/VecPar fails at LLVM lowering, regardless of parallelization. This is a fundamental limitation of the current lowering pipeline.
- **ParTile/ParThreads + Tiling + VecSeq** is the most reliable high-performance pattern (scf.forall + scf.for + vector ops lower cleanly).
- **VecPar as terminal** works well after ParThreads and LoopInterchange but NOT after ParTile (postcondition failure).
- **Packing** provides moderate cache improvement (~5x) but doesn't combine well with vectorization.

## Synthesis

### Execution-Aware Composability Rules
1. **Promotion + Vectorization = LLVM failure** (any order, with or without parallelization)
2. **VecPar after Tiling = massive regression** (too many tiny parallel tasks)
3. **VecSeq/VecPar after ParTile = parameter-dependent.** Succeeds with vector_sizes=[4,4,4], fails with [8,8,8]. The matmul inside scf.forall IS accessible to vectorization with smaller vector sizes.
4. **VecSeq/VecPar are terminal** — no further transformations possible
5. **Unrolling is near-terminal** — only Tiling can follow

### Selected Schedule Shapes for RL Training
1. **[VectorizationPar]** — 1-step, best for small/medium
2. **[ParallelizationTile, VectorizationPar]** — 2-step, strong across all sizes (2.4ms medium, 3.3ms large)
3. **[ParallelizationTile, VectorizationSeq]** — 2-step, strong across all sizes (2.6ms medium, 3.8ms large)
4. **[ParallelizationTile, Tiling, VectorizationSeq]** — 3-step, best for large (2.0ms), robust across sizes
5. **[ParallelizationTile, Promotion]** — 2-step, good all-around
6. **[ParallelizationThreads, Tiling, VectorizationSeq]** — 3-step, strong alternative
7. **[ParallelizationThreads, VectorizationPar]** — 2-step, compact and effective
8. **[ParallelizationTile, Tiling, Promotion]** — 3-step, decent for small/medium (avoid for large)
