### Phase 1 Key Observations
- **Parallelization** is the dominant single optimization (15-25x speedup), with finer granularity [8,8] being best (1.68ms, 25x).
- **Im2col** converts the 1x1 conv to batched matmul and achieves 1.55x speedup alone.
- **Vectorization** provides marginal improvement (1.2x).
- **Loop interchange** is detrimental (0.9x, slower than base).
- **Packing, padding, peeling** fail postcondition on this kernel.
- Tiling, promotion, unrolling, distribution, fusion, canonicalization, bufferization are essentially no-ops for this kernel by themselves.

### Composability Issues Discovered

1. **Im2col breaks tag contract**: After im2col lowering, `tag = "operation_0"` migrates to `tensor.expand_shape` instead of a linalg op. This causes all subsequent actions that target the tagged linalg op to fail postcondition.

2. **Interchange invalidates im2col**: Loop interchange converts `linalg.conv_2d_nchw_fchw` to `linalg.generic`, but im2col requires a `linalg.conv_2d_*` op specifically. Precondition fails.

3. **Vectorization invalidates im2col**: Vectorization decomposes the conv_2d into vector ops + linalg.generic, consuming the conv_2d op. Im2col cannot find a conv_2d target.

4. **Promotion + im2col bufferization conflict**: The combination passes all transformation-level checks but fails during one-shot bufferization at runtime due to `scf.yield` operand equivalence violation.

5. **Packing, padding, peeling**: All fail postcondition on this 1x1 conv kernel, likely because the spatial dimensions (KH=1, KW=1) are too small for these transformations to apply meaningfully.

### Performance Analysis
- The gap to PyTorch (0.34x) is significant. PyTorch likely benefits from:
  - Optimized BLAS backend (MKL/OpenBLAS) with highly tuned 1x1 conv -> GEMM lowering
  - f32 computation (vs our f64)
  - Memory layout optimizations
  - Micro-kernel assembly for AVX2
- Our best MLIR schedule achieves ~119 GFLOPS (102.7M FLOPs / 0.866ms), while PyTorch achieves ~348 GFLOPS.

### Ordering Constraints
1. **Im2col must come last** in any schedule (it breaks the tag for subsequent actions).
2. **Parallelization should come first** (it's the most impactful and composes well with most subsequent actions).
3. **Vectorization and im2col are mutually exclusive** (both consume the conv_2d op).
4. **Interchange must NOT precede im2col** (converts conv_2d to generic, breaking im2col precondition).
5. **Canonical schedule pattern**: `parallel -> [optional tiling] -> im2col`
