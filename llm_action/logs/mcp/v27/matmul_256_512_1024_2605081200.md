### Kernel-Specific Notes
- Large kernel (256x512x1024, 268M FLOPs) — best absolute speedup of all 4 kernels
- 3-step schedule outperforms 2-step by ~12%: extra tiling improves cache utilization on larger problem
- All dimensions are powers of 2, no dynamic-dimension vectorization issues
- ParallelDirect single action already gives 15.2x, best among all kernels (more work per thread)
