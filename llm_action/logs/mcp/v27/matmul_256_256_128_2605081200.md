### Kernel-Specific Notes
- Small kernel (256x256x128, 8.4M FLOPs) — parallelization overhead is significant relative to compute
- 2-step schedule (ParDirect->Vec) outperforms 3-step (ParDirect->Tile->Vec) because extra tiling adds overhead on small problem
- All dimensions are powers of 2, no dynamic-dimension vectorization issues
- At 0.22ms, approaching parallelization overhead floor
