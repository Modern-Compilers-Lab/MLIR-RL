### Notes
- Tiling, ParTile, ParThreads are universally composable as first actions (all 8 successors OK).

- Promotion bufferizes to memref and adds alloca/copy scaffolding; Im2col/VecSeq/VecPar fail postcondition on the bufferized form.
- Im2col/VecSeq/VecPar produce identical output (im2col + matmul-like generic), so self-blocking for Im2col (no conv to lower again).
- VecSeq/VecPar CAN follow Im2col (vectorizing the matmul-like generic works).

## Phase 3b: LoopInterchange Deep Dive (on K1, K2, K3)

### K1 (3x3 conv) — LI[N↔C] is BENEFICIAL
The permutation [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] (swap N↔C) provides significant speedup after ParTile:

| # | Schedule | Permutation | Time (ms) | Speedup | vs non-LI equiv |
|---|----------|-------------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | ParTile → LI → Promotion | [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] | 1.53 | 52.2x | 1.50x better than ParTile→Promo (2.30ms) |
| 2 | ParTile → LI → Tiling → Promotion | [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] | 1.54 | 51.8x | new 4-step path |
| 3 | ParTile → LI | [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] | 1.69 | 47.2x | 1.78x better than ParTile alone (3.01ms) |
| 4 | ParTile → LI → Tiling | [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] | 1.69 | 47.2x | 1.23x better than ParTile→Tile (2.08ms) |
| 5 | ParTile → LI → ParThreads | [4,1,2,3,0,5,6] | 1.70 | 46.9x | strong new path |

**Why N↔C helps for K1:** With N=C=128, swapping batch (N) and channel (C) dimensions improves data locality for reduction iteration within parallelized tiles. Each thread processes a smaller N-slice with contiguous C-iteration.

Other permutations ([1,0,2,3,4,5,6] N↔F, [0,1,2,3,5,4,6] C↔KH, etc.) provide zero benefit on K1.

### K2 (1x1 small) — LI does NOT help
No permutation improves any schedule. Best LI-containing schedule (ParTile→LI→Tiling: 0.487ms) matches but does not beat the non-LI best (ParTile→Tiling: 0.477ms). Root cause: 1x1 conv with small C=32 has natural loop order already optimal.

### K3 (1x1 large) — LI does NOT help
Best LI schedule (ParTile→LI[N↔C]→Tiling: 19.14ms) is 1.9x worse than non-LI best (ParTile→Tiling→Im2col: 10.01ms). LI blocks Im2col, which is the critical accelerator for this kernel.

### LI Summary
- **LI[N↔C] is valuable for conv2d kernels where N≈C (both large)** — improves data locality
- **LI is not helpful for 1x1 convs** — trivial spatial kernel dims make loop reordering irrelevant
- **LI must come AFTER ParTile** — the reverse order (LI→ParTile) is much worse
- **LI and Im2col are mutually exclusive paths** — LI blocks Im2col, so schedules must choose one

## Phase 5: ParThreads Deep Dive (RL-discovered pattern)

The free RL agent (no schedule graph) discovered: **ParTile → ParThreads → LI → Promotion → Tiling** at 1.46ms on K1.
Key insight: **ParThreads in position 2** creates nested parallelism (outer forall tiles + inner forall threads).

**Critical findings:**

- Im2col path FAILS with ParThreads due to VecSeq compile errors (vector.mask on dynamic shapes)

### Key Insights
- **ParThreads(4) in position 2 is a universal amplifier** — improves every path by adding nested parallelism
- **The RL-discovered skeleton is the overall best** for K1 (72.5x), surpassing both Im2col (61.2x) and previous LI paths (52.2x)
- **Two complementary optimization paths**: LI+Promotion (best for 3x3 conv with N≈C) vs Im2col (best for 1x1 conv)
- **ParThreads(4) is the sweet spot** — 14+ threads create dynamic shapes that break subsequent optimizations
- **Multi-level tiling with Promotion sandwiched is a new K2 winner** (Phase 7)
- **Best schedules now reach 73-83% of PyTorch** performance (K1: 0.79x PyTorch; K3: 0.26x PyTorch)

## Phase 7: Repeated Actions Exploration

Investigated whether applying the same action multiple times (multi-tiling, multi-promotion) improves performance.

### Findings

**Double Promotion: IMPOSSIBLE**
- Second Promotion fails precondition on alloca buffers created by first Promotion.
- Structurally blocked: the tool expects strided subviews from the original memory hierarchy, not contiguous alloca buffers.

**Double Tiling: K2 IMPROVEMENT, K1/K3 NO IMPROVEMENT**

K1 (3x3 conv):
- ParTile→ParThreads→LI→Promo→Tile→Tile: No improvement over single Tile (1.10ms). Added overhead from second tiling level.

K2 (1x1 conv):
| # | Schedule | Time (ms) | Speedup vs Base | vs prev best (0.451) |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **ParTile→Tile→Promo→Tile** | **0.396** | **32.4x** | **1.14x better** |
| 2 | ParTile→ParThreads→Im2col→Tile→VecSeq | 0.399 | 32.2x | 1.13x better |
| 3 | ParTile→ParThreads→Im2col→VecSeq | 0.451 | 28.5x | baseline |

K3 (1x1 large):
- No improvement. ParTile→ParThreads→Im2col at 9.10ms remains best.

### Changes Made
1. **Tiling.unique_execution → False**: Allows multi-level tiling (MAX_ACTION_EXECUTIONS=2). The docstring already documented this as "a key tuning knob for convolutions."
2. **SCHEDULE_GRAPH updated**:
   - Replaced `ParTile→Tiling` with `ParTile→Tiling→Promotion→Tiling` (K2: 0.396ms vs 0.477ms, 17% improvement)
   - Replaced `ParTile→ParThreads→Im2col→VecSeq` with `ParTile→ParThreads→Im2col→Tiling→VecSeq` (K2: 0.399ms vs 0.451ms, 12% improvement)
