### Key Composability Observations
1. **Vectorization**: Always fails postcondition on conv_2d_nchw_fchw regardless of prior transforms. Structurally incompatible with this kernel type.
2. **Packing**: Fails postcondition on raw conv and after most transforms. **Only succeeds after Im2colLowering** which converts to a batched matmul-like generic.
3. **Im2colLowering**: Requires `linalg.conv_2d_nchw_fchw` op (fails precondition after LoopInterchange which converts to generic, or after itself).
4. **Promotion**: Terminal action — converts to memref form. Im2col fails post on promoted code. Most other actions still work after Promotion.
5. **ParallelTiling/Parallelization**: Compose freely with all actions except Vec/Pack.
