## Phase 4: Local Tuning Summary

Best schedule: **ParallelVectorization[8,8,8]** at **0.28ms** (147.6x vs MLIR base, 0.47x vs PyTorch)

Vector size [8,8,8] outperforms [4,4,4] (0.28ms vs 0.29ms) due to larger SIMD utilization on AVX2.
