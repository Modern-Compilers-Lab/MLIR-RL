**Key observations:**
- **Vec and ParVec SUCCEED on linalg.generic** (unlike named ops like conv/pooling). This is because relu is already a linalg.generic with simple identity indexing maps.
- Vectorization alone is **5x slower** (20.33ms vs 3.84ms base) due to loop overhead with small spatial dims (7x7 → step=1 inner loops).
- ParallelVectorization (1.25ms) is much better than plain Vectorization because it parallelizes across all dims into scf.forall.
- **DirectParallelization[16] is the best single action**: 1.17ms (3.28x speedup, 0.62x vs PyTorch).
- Im2colLowering **FAILS precondition** (requires conv_2d_nchw_fchw).
- Tiling, LoopInterchange, LoopUnrolling provide no benefit on this elementwise kernel.

**Key finding:** No multi-step schedule improves over single DirectParallelization. Adding vectorization after parallelization introduces loop overhead without benefit for this elementwise kernel with small spatial dims (7x7).

## Phase 4: Local Tuning Summary

Best schedule: **DirectParallelization[16]** at **1.17ms** (3.28x vs MLIR base, 0.62x vs PyTorch)

For elementwise relu, simple parallelization across the batch dimension is most effective. The 128x512x7x7 tensor (3.2M elements) is large enough to benefit from parallelism but the operation is too simple for vectorization overhead to pay off.
